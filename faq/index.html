Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about building with Nexcon.

How much does it cost to build a custom home?

The cost depends on the site, size, design, materials, specifications and level of finish. Contact us with your requirements and we can discuss the appropriate next steps.

Can I use my own architect?

Yes. We can work with your existing architect and consultant team, subject to project requirements.

Can Nexcon manage the builder for me?

Our construction management services can provide coordination, monitoring, inspections and reporting. The exact scope depends on your project and engagement.

Do you offer knockdown rebuild services?

Yes. We can assist with the planning and coordination of knockdown rebuild projects.

Do you provide building inspections?

Yes. Nexcon provides construction-stage and practical completion inspections across Victoria.

What building inspections do you offer?

We offer pre-purchase inspections, construction-stage inspections (including frame and lock-up inspections), practical completion inspections and defect inspections.

What does an inspection report include?

Our reports can include photographs, observations, identified defects, areas requiring attention, recommendations and construction-stage information.

Do you offer fixed-price homes?

Selected house and land packages are offered at fixed prices, subject to the specific package's inclusions and exclusions.

What areas do you service?

Our focus is Melbourne, particularly Melbourne's eastern suburbs, with selected projects throughout Victoria.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes. Our Projects page shows selected Nexcon projects and how we approach design, construction, coordination and problem-solving.

How do I get started?

Contact us with your project details. We'll arrange an initial discussion to understand your requirements and explain the next steps.
