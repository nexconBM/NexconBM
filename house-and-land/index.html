House & land packages

Buying land and building a home can involve hundreds of decisions. Our house and land packages simplify the process by bringing the land and home component together.

Nexcon works with selected land development partners across Melbourne and regional Victoria to provide practical house and land options.

Fixed-price options

Where specified, our house and land packages are offered at a fixed price, giving you greater certainty when planning your investment.

Your package can include:

  • Land
  • Home design
  • Construction
  • Standard inclusions
  • Selected upgrades
  • Relevant project coordination

Specific inclusions, exclusions and pricing vary between packages.
