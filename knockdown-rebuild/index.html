Knockdown rebuild

Sometimes the best place for your next home is the place you're already living.

A knockdown rebuild allows you to keep the location you love while creating a home designed for the way you live today.

From existing property to new home

Nexcon can help coordinate the key stages of your project:

  1. 1

    Existing property assessment

    Understand the site and existing conditions.

  2. 2

    Demolition planning

    Coordinate the requirements for safely removing the existing dwelling.

  3. 3

    Design

    Develop a new home around the site, lifestyle and budget.

  4. 4

    Approvals

    Coordinate the relevant planning, building and consultant requirements.

  5. 5

    Construction

    Manage the construction process through to completion.

  6. 6

    Handover

    Complete inspections, defects and final handover.

Why knockdown rebuild?

You can:

  • Stay in a location you already love
  • Create a modern floor plan
  • Improve energy efficiency
  • Increase functionality
  • Add features that suit your family
  • Design for your long-term needs
