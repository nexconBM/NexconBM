Renovation & extension

A renovation can add comfort, function and value to a property, but only when it is planned and managed well.

Nexcon helps owners plan and manage renovation and extension work on existing and investment properties, from small refreshes to larger upgrades and additions.

What we do

Scope & budget planning

Define what the renovation should achieve and what it should cost before work begins.

Kitchen & bathroom updates

Plan and coordinate kitchen and bathroom refreshes and upgrades.

Paint, flooring & finishes

Refresh walls, floors and finishes to lift the look and feel of the property.

Layout & fit-out changes

Adjust layouts, joinery and fit-out so the property works better for its use.

Pre-sale & pre-lease refreshes

Targeted updates to prepare a property before it is sold or leased.

Coordination & reporting

We coordinate the trades and keep you updated with clear progress reports.

Extensions & additions

Plan and coordinate ground-floor extensions and second-storey additions.

Structural alterations

Plan and coordinate structural changes to the existing building, such as removing or relocating walls.

Plan the work before you spend the money.
