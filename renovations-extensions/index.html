    Renovate

    Often a good fit when the home has enough space but the kitchen, bathrooms, layout or finishes need work.

    Extend

    Often a good fit when you like the location and the home but need more room, on the ground floor or with a second storey.

    Renovation questions

    Is renovating the same as remodelling?

    Largely, yes. Most Australians say renovation, while remodelling is often used for changes to a home's layout or design. We can talk through either kind of work with you.

    Do I need a building permit for a renovation or extension?

    It depends on the work. In Victoria, extensions, second-storey additions and structural changes generally need a building permit, while cosmetic work such as painting often does not. A registered building surveyor or your council can confirm what applies to your property.

    Do you work on investment properties?

    Yes. We help with renovation and extension work on existing and investment properties, including targeted updates before a property is sold or leased.

    What should I have ready for our first conversation?

    The property address, some photos, any existing plans, what you would like to change, and a rough budget and timing if you have them. It is fine if you are still working these out.
