    Property management

    Owning a property means keeping on top of repairs, inspections, trades and decisions, often while you are busy with everything else.

    Nexcon provides building-focused property management support to help owners keep their properties in good condition and their records organised.

    What we do

    Property condition reviews

    Regular reviews of the building's condition, with photographs and notes on anything that needs attention.

    Maintenance planning

    A practical plan for upcoming repairs and upkeep, so cost and timing are not a surprise.

    Trades coordination

    We organise and coordinate the trades needed for repairs and upkeep.

    Quotation review

    Compare quotations for repair and upgrade work and understand what is included.

    Reporting & records

    Clear reports with photographs, so you know what was done and what comes next.

    One point of contact

    One team to deal with, instead of chasing several tradespeople yourself.

    One point of contact for your property.
