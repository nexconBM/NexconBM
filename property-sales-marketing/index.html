Property sales & marketing

Selling a property, whether it is an investment or your own home, works best with the right strategy, the right presentation and the right agent relationships.

Nexcon coordinates the sale process on your behalf, connecting you with trusted agents and helping you present your property to the market with confidence.

What we do

Agent selection

Help you choose an agent with the right experience for your property and area.

Campaign strategy

Review marketing timing, channels and presentation options with you.

Property presentation

Advice on styling, photography and preparing the property for sale.

Pricing guidance

Help you understand comparable sales and realistic price expectations.

Contract coordination

Connect you with the right professionals to review contract terms.

Enquiry updates

Keep you informed as buyer enquiries and offers come in.

Negotiation support

Support you through offers, negotiation and settlement terms.

Settlement coordination

Help coordinate the steps through to settlement.

Who is this for?

Our property sales & marketing service is particularly suited to clients who:

  • Are selling an investment property
  • Are selling their own home
  • Are building or adjusting a property portfolio
  • Want a single point of contact through the sale
  • Want independent guidance alongside their agent
  • Are relocating interstate or overseas

We help you approach the sale process with clarity, so you understand your options at every stage.
