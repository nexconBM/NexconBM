    Construction project management

    Building a home can become overwhelming when you are expected to coordinate the builder, architect, engineers, consultants, suppliers and trades yourself.

    Nexcon provides construction project management support to help homeowners stay informed and organised throughout the build.

    What we do

    Builder & contract review

    We help you understand builder proposals, contracts, inclusions and construction requirements.

    Quotation review

    Compare quotations and identify differences in scope, inclusions and pricing.

    Consultant coordination

    Coordinate communication between architects, engineers, surveyors and other consultants.

    Construction monitoring

    Monitor key stages of construction and identify issues that require attention.

    Site inspections

    Attend agreed construction stages and provide inspection reports with photographs.

    Progress reporting

    Provide clear updates so you understand what has been completed and what comes next.

    Variation review

    Help review proposed variations and understand their scope and cost.

    Handover support

    Assist with final inspections, defects and practical completion.

    Who is this for?

    Our project management service is particularly suited to homeowners who:

    • Are building a high-value custom home
    • Have limited time to manage the project themselves
    • Want dedicated construction oversight
    • Want help comparing builders
    • Want someone experienced to coordinate the project team
    • Want clearer reporting during construction

    Your project. Your decisions. Our support.

    Nexcon does not replace your decisions. We provide the construction knowledge, coordination and reporting you need to make informed decisions.
