    How we work

    From your idea to your finished project.

    1. 1

      Consultation

      We start by understanding your property, lifestyle, budget and project objectives.

    2. 2

      Planning

      We review the site and project requirements and identify the key steps involved.

    3. 3

      Design & approvals

      Work with the right design and consulting professionals on the plans and approvals your project needs.

    4. 4

      Pricing

      Review the scope, specifications and costs so you understand what you're committing to.

    5. 5

      Construction

      Our team coordinates the construction process and keeps you informed as the project progresses.

    6. 6

      Completion & handover

      We work through the final checks and completion requirements, then hand the finished work back to you.

    Renovating or building should be exciting, not overwhelming.

    A renovation or new home involves architects, engineers, builders, trades, suppliers, approvals, inspections and hundreds of decisions. Nexcon helps bring those moving parts together.

    Long-term thinking
