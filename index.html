    How we work

    From your idea to your finished home.

    1. 1

      Consultation

      We start by understanding your property, lifestyle, budget and project objectives.

    2. 2

      Planning

      We review the site and project requirements and identify the key steps involved.

    3. 3

      Design

      Work with the appropriate design and consulting professionals to develop your home.

    4. 4

      Pricing

      Review the scope, specifications and costs so you understand what you're committing to.

    5. 5

      Construction

      Our team coordinates the construction process and keeps you informed as the project progresses.

    6. 6

      Inspection & handover

      We work through the final stages, inspections and completion requirements so your new home is ready for you.
