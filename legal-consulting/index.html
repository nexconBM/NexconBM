Legal consulting

Buying, selling or building a property involves contracts and legal steps that are easy to overlook without the right guidance.

Nexcon is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. We connect you with independent, qualified legal professionals and help coordinate that advice alongside the rest of your project, so nothing falls through the cracks.

What we help coordinate

Contract of sale review

Connect you with a solicitor or conveyancer to review contract terms before you sign.

Building contract review

Help arrange review of your building contract and special conditions.

Due diligence

Coordinate title, zoning and planning checks with the right professional.

Settlement support

Help keep the legal and settlement timeline on track.

Easements & covenants

Arrange advice on easements, covenants and title restrictions affecting your site.

Dispute referral

Refer you to appropriate legal support if a dispute arises during your project.

Who is this for?

Our legal consulting coordination is particularly suited to clients who are:

  • Buying or selling a property
  • Signing a building or renovation contract
  • Purchasing land with title conditions they don't understand
  • Dealing with a boundary, easement or covenant issue
  • Wanting a second set of eyes before they sign
  • Managing a dispute during construction

Know what you're signing.
