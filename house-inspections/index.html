When to book each

Building Inspection or PCI, which one you need

Building Inspection

Suited to an existing home, whether you're buying one, checking on a property you already own, or getting a second opinion before renovating.

Practical Completion (PCI) Inspection

Booked once a new build or major renovation is finished, before you sign off with the builder, so any defects are raised while they're still the builder's responsibility to fix.

What's included

Every part of the home, photographed and documented

Areas we check

Every habitable room and service area of the home: entry, hallways, living and dining areas, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, ensuite, walk-in robe, laundry, WC, roof space, subfloor, garage or carport, verandah, and outdoor areas.

What we look for

General wear and cracking, rust on structural steel, timber rot, loose or unstable brickwork, doors and windows that don't open or close properly, and blocked subfloor ventilation, documented with photos as we go.
