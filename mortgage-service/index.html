Mortgage service

Financing a home or investment property is easier with the right guidance from the start.

Nexcon is not a lender or credit provider. We connect you with trusted, independent mortgage brokers and help coordinate finance timing alongside your purchase or build, so your loan and your project stay in step.

What we help coordinate

Broker introduction

Connect you with a mortgage broker suited to your situation.

Pre-approval guidance

Help you understand what pre-approval means for your budget and timeline.

Loan comparison support

Help you prepare the right questions when comparing loan options.

Construction loan timing

Coordinate finance drawdowns with your build or settlement schedule.

Investment lending

Connect investors with brokers experienced in investment property lending.

Refinance guidance

Help you understand when refinancing may suit your circumstances.

Who is this for?

Our mortgage service coordination is particularly suited to:

  • First home buyers
  • Property investors
  • Clients building a new home or house and land package
  • Clients who want finance coordinated with construction
  • Clients considering refinancing
  • Clients who want an independent second opinion
