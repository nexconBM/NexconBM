    Building inspections

    A new home is made up of thousands of components. An inspection at the right stage can help identify construction issues before they become difficult or expensive to address.

    Nexcon provides building inspection services across Victoria.

    Inspection services

    Pre-purchase inspections

    Understand the condition of an existing property before making a purchase decision.

    Construction-stage inspections

    Inspect important stages of a new build as construction progresses.

    Frame inspection

    Review framing before subsequent construction stages cover the structure.

    Lock-up inspection

    Assess the property when the building envelope is substantially enclosed.

    Practical completion inspection

    Identify outstanding works and defects before final completion.

    Defect inspections

    Document defects and items requiring rectification.

    How it works

    1. 1

      Choose your inspection

      An existing Building Inspection, or a Practical Completion (PCI) Inspection for a newly built home.

    2. 2

      We walk the site

      An inspector checks structure, compliance, and finish against the approved plans and Australian Standards.

    3. 3

      You get a report

      Issues are documented with photos, so you know exactly what to raise before you sign off.

    Inspection reports

    Our reports can include:

    • Photographs
    • Observations
    • Identified defects
    • Areas requiring attention
    • Recommendations
    • Construction-stage information

    What's included

    Every part of the home, photographed and documented

    Areas we check

    Every habitable room and service area of the home: entry, hallways, living and dining areas, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, ensuite, walk-in robe, laundry, WC, roof space, subfloor, garage or carport, verandah, and outdoor areas.

    What we look for

    General wear and cracking, rust on structural steel, timber rot, loose or unstable brickwork, doors and windows that don't open or close properly, and blocked subfloor ventilation, documented with photos as we go.
