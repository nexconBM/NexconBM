    About Nexcon

    Building a home is about more than bricks, concrete and finishes. It is about creating a place that works for your family, your lifestyle and your future.

    Nexcon Building Management was established to provide homeowners with a more practical and coordinated approach to residential construction. We combine construction knowledge, project coordination and building inspection experience to help our clients make better decisions throughout their project.

    Engineer-led

    Your project is overseen by a registered civil engineer.

    Nexcon is led by Kevin Chan, a qualified civil engineer. The structural side of your project, from footings and framing to drainage and the parts of a home you can't see once it's finished, is checked by someone trained to understand it.

    Our approach

    Good building starts with good communication.

    A successful project depends on many people working together. Architects. Engineers. Builders. Trades. Suppliers. Consultants. And, most importantly, the homeowner.

    Our role is to help connect those moving parts. We aim to make the construction process easier to understand, easier to manage and more transparent for our clients.
